You’re speeding down the highway towards a looming corporate mega building. Each minute you take is docked from your pay at the end of the job, simply delivering some late night food. Two cars burst from the on-ramp, gunshots blaring. You speed up, not wanting to get in the middle of some gang feud. One of the vehicles flies past you, rogue bullets shattering your rear windows. The car swerves in front of you trying to avoid its pursuers. Inertia slams you against the driver side door as the pursuing car rams against you, flipping your car. Hung upside down, blood rushing to your head, the dark highway becomes suddenly illuminated as the pursuing car hits the gas tank of the other blooming a ball of fire. The assailant's roaring engine fades into the distance. Ears ringing, an even brighter source of light descends from the sky. A blue projection outlining a landing pad appears on the road in front of you. The words warning - landing, flash on the road, and soon a Trauma Team aircraft drops just ahead of you. Relief courses through you as heavily armored personnel rush to your vehicle. One of them kneels down in front of your car, scanning your face. “Non-client here,” their voice buzzes, “locate platinum client.” They step away from you, turning to the blaze. A singed body is carried out by two other responders while a third attempts resuscitation. The client is lifted into the armored vehicle as the words back away - back away blare on loop in an automated monotonous drone. It lifts back in the air as you shout to them before blacking out.
